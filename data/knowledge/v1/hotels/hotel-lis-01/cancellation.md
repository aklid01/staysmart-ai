## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 2 days before check-in. Cancel at any point before that deadline and nothing is charged; cancellations after the deadline are charged for the first night of the stay.

## How to cancel

Cancel through the same channel used to book, or contact reception directly. A written confirmation with a cancellation reference is sent for every cancellation.

## Refund process

When a charged stay is released and resold, the corresponding amount is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds usually appear within a few business days, depending on the card issuer.

## No-show policy

Guests who do not arrive and do not cancel are treated as a late cancellation and charged for the first night.
