## About the hotel

Alfama Riverside Boutique is a 4-star boutique hotel in Alfama, Lisbon. It is a small riverside property within walking distance of the Alfama viewpoints, and the house style is intimate rather than large: a dozen rooms across two categories, a ground-floor restaurant and a quiet lounge.

## Location and neighbourhood

The hotel sits in Alfama, the oldest district of the city. Steep lanes, miradouros and the riverfront promenade are all a short walk away, and the tram stops nearby for trips further uphill.

## Good to know

The property suits couples and solo travellers who prefer character over scale. Reception can arrange luggage assistance, late arrival instructions and taxi bookings on request.
