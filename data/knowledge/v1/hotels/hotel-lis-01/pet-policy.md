## Pets at Alfama Riverside Boutique

Pets are not allowed at this property. The rooms are compact and the house prefers to keep the lounge and restaurant fur-free for all guests.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are welcome where required by law; mention this when booking so the room can be prepared.

## Nearby options

Guests travelling with animals can ask reception about local boarding services in Lisbon; the team will share general guidance on request.
