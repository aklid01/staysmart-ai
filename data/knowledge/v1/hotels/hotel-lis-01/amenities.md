## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi covers every room and the lounge at no charge. Each morning the kitchen serves a breakfast with pastries, fruit and eggs, and the ground-floor restaurant serves Portuguese dinner dishes in the evening. All rooms and shared areas have air conditioning.

## Comfort details

Guests can borrow an umbrella, a travel adapter or a city guide at reception. Rooms are cleaned daily, and the lounge keeps a small library of books about Lisbon.

## Getting around

Alfama is best explored on foot. The tram stop a few minutes downhill connects to the rest of the city, and reception will happily point out the walk to the viewpoints.
