## Standard Double

The Standard Double sleeps up to 2 guests in a queen bed. It is a calm, compact room with tiled floors, warm lighting, wi-fi and air conditioning, and a small writing desk under the window.

## River Suite

The River Suite sleeps up to 3 guests in a king bed. It looks over the water with a sitting corner, a larger bathroom, wi-fi, air conditioning, and breakfast served in the suite on request.

## Which to choose

Choose the Standard Double for short city stays and the River Suite for longer visits, special occasions, or when a view matters most.
