## Wellness

The on-site spa offers soaking baths and booked massage treatments in a cedar-lined room below the main house. Guests alternate between the garden, the river walk and the spa through the afternoon.

## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi reaches every room. Breakfast is a formal Japanese morning set served in the room, and the house restaurant serves the seasonal kaiseki dinner, with in-room table service on request. The historic wing cools naturally with shutters, fans and evening garden breezes, which is part of the traditional stay rather than a missing convenience.

## Services

Reception arranges temple transfers on foot, umbrellas for the rainy season, and a tea corner open through the afternoon.
