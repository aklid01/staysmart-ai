## Garden Ryokan Room

The Garden Ryokan Room sleeps up to 2 guests on futon bedding over tatami, facing the garden. The room includes wi-fi, a breakfast served in the room each morning, and access to the spa below the main house.

## The ryokan rhythm

Staff lay the futons out after the kaiseki dinner and put them away each morning, turning the same room into a day lounge. Tea arrives with the evening service.

## What to expect

Walls are paper and wood, voices carry gently, and the garden soundtrack is the point. Guests wanting hotel-style room service should choose a city property instead.
