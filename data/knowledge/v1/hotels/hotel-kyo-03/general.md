## About the hotel

Arashiyama Ryokan is a 5-star traditional ryokan in the Arashiyama district of Kyoto. It holds the highest guest rating in the collection, with garden views, an on-site spa and kaiseki dinner service in a small, quiet house.

## Location and neighbourhood

Arashiyama is the bamboo grove and river valley on Kyoto's western edge. Morning walks through the grove, the river bridge and the temple paths start at the ryokan's gate.

## Good to know

The ryokan suits travellers celebrating something or slowing down at the end of a trip. Dinner is served in the room, and the evening routine follows the traditional ryokan rhythm rather than hotel time.
