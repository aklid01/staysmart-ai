## Pets at Arashiyama Ryokan

Pets are not allowed at this property. Tatami rooms, shared paper walls and the formal dining service make the traditional ryokan format unsuitable for animals.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are welcome where required by law; notify the ryokan in advance so the room preparation can account for it.

## Nearby options

Reception can suggest licensed boarding providers in the Arashiyama area for guests travelling with animals.
