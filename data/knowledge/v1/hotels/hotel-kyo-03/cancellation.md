## Cancellation terms

Bookings at Arashiyama Ryokan are non-refundable. The ryokan rate covers a fixed kaiseki dinner and breakfast service, prepared against the reservation, so the booking cannot be cancelled or refunded.

## Why the rate is strict

The house has only four rooms and plans its kitchen purchases around confirmed bookings. This is standard practice for small ryokan properties in Japan.

## Date changes

Moving a booking to different dates counts as a modification and is not possible on this rate. A new booking can be made separately if plans change.
