## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free across the lofts. A small gym with cardio machines and a rack of weights sits on the lower level, and secure on-site parking is offered in the garage behind the building. Dedicated pet-friendly floors welcome animals in selected suites.

## Services

A shared laundry room runs on app payment, the front desk holds parcels for long-stay guests, and a binder of neighbourhood walking maps lives in every suite.

## Getting around

The streetcar and bus stops are at the corner, bike-share docks stand outside, and the walk to the waterfront markets takes about fifteen minutes.
