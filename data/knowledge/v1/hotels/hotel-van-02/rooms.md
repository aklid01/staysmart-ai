## Loft Suite

The Loft Suite sleeps up to 2 guests in a queen bed under the mezzanine. It is a full loft with a kitchenette, a sofa corner, wi-fi and a pet-friendly floor assignment on request.

## Living style

The kitchenette covers simple meals: two burners, a fridge and a coffee maker. Guests staying a week or more tend to use it every morning.

## Practical notes

Housekeeping runs every second day for long stays, spare towels live in the closet, and the building entrance has a coded door after hours.
