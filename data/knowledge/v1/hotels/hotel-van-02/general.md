## About the hotel

Granville Loft Suites is a 3-star property of converted loft suites on a heritage street in Vancouver. The house keeps exposed brick, high windows and a small gym, and dedicates whole floors to pet-friendly stays.

## Location and neighbourhood

The street runs through the gallery and entertainment district, with restaurants and live venues nearby. Downtown Vancouver and the bridges to the south are both an easy walk.

## Good to know

The house suits longer visits, guests with animals, and travellers who prefer apartment-style living over a classic hotel box.
