## Pets at Granville Loft Suites

Pets are allowed at this property. Whole floors are dedicated pet-friendly suites, each with hard flooring, an easy-wash rug and direct lift access to the street for late walks.

## Rules and comfort

Animals stay supervised in the hallways, sleep on their own bedding, and are never left alone in the suite for full days. A water bowl and a floor mat are in every pet-friendly suite.

## Practical notes

Mention the animal when booking so a pet-friendly floor is assigned. A cleaning surcharge may apply and is confirmed at the front desk before arrival.
