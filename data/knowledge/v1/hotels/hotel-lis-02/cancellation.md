## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 1 day before check-in. Cancel before the deadline and nothing is charged; later cancellations are charged for the first night of the stay.

## How to cancel

Cancel through the booking channel or by contacting reception. Every cancellation receives a written confirmation with a reference number.

## Refund process

Refunds for prepaid bookings are returned to the original payment method once the cancellation is confirmed. Processing usually takes a few business days.

## No-show policy

A no-show without cancellation is charged for the first night and the remaining nights are released.
