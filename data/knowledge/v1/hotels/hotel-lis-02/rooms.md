## Standard Twin

The Standard Twin sleeps up to 2 guests in twin beds that can be prepared as a double bed on request. It is a practical room with wi-fi, air conditioning, a work desk and blackout curtains.

## Best for

Solo travellers, colleagues sharing a room, and couples who prefer separate beds all use this category. Rooms on the upper floors are quieter than street-side ones.

## Practical notes

Housekeeping runs daily, extra pillows and blankets are at the front desk, and rooms are cleaned between 10:00 and 14:00.
