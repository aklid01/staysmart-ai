## Pets at Baixa Central Hotel

Pets are not allowed at this property. The house keeps rooms allergen-friendly for the many business guests who rotate through the building.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are accommodated where required by law. Flag this at booking so the room assignment can be adjusted.

## Nearby options

Reception keeps a list of licensed boarding and day-care providers in Lisbon for guests travelling with animals.
