## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free across the building. A small gym with cardio machines and free weights is open from early morning to late evening, and covered on-site parking is offered to guests arriving by car. All rooms have air conditioning.

## Services

Reception runs a luggage room, dry-cleaning drop-off and a small shop with travel basics. Meeting-style work corners with power sockets line the lobby.

## Getting around

The metro and bus stops are a short walk from the door, taxis rank around the corner, and the airport transfer by taxi takes roughly twenty minutes.
