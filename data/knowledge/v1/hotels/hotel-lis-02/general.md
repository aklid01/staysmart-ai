## About the hotel

Baixa Central Hotel is a 3-star business hotel in the Baixa district of Lisbon. With nearly a thousand reviews behind it, the house focuses on reliability: quick check-in, quiet rooms and a handy position for getting around the city.

## Location and neighbourhood

Baixa is the flat, grid-planned heart of Lisbon. Transit links, shops and cafes are on the doorstep, and the riverfront is a short walk downhill.

## Good to know

The hotel suits business travellers and city breakers who want practicality. Front desk staff speak Portuguese and English, and printed city maps are available at reception.
