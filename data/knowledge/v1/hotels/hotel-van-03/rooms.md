## Park View Double

The Park View Double sleeps up to 2 guests in a queen bed. It is a timber room with wi-fi, breakfast included for the whole party, and a pet-friendly floor assignment on request; selected rooms of this category open towards the park canopy.

## Room rhythm

Mornings start early here: runners leave at dawn, and the breakfast room fills quickly after seven. Guests booking this room should expect a lively, outdoorsy house rather than a quiet business floor.

## Practical notes

Towels for after-seawall showers are stocked generously, boot brushes stand by the entrance, and housekeeping keeps a daily towel refresh for long stays.
