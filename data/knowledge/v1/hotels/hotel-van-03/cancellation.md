## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 7 days before check-in. Cancellations inside that window are charged less a 2-night penalty, taken from the total when the cancellation is processed.

## How to cancel

Cancel through the booking channel or the front desk. The written confirmation lists the cancellation reference and any penalty applied.

## Refund process

Refunds return to the original payment method after processing. The penalty, when it applies, appears as a separate line on the confirmation.

## No-show policy

A no-show is charged the two-night penalty and the remaining nights are released to the lodge inventory.
