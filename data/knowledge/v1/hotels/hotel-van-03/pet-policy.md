## Pets at Stanley Park Lodge

Pets are allowed at this property. Dedicated pet-friendly floors, a rinse station at the parking gate and a restaurant patio that welcomes animals make the lodge one of the easiest stays in the collection for travellers with dogs.

## Rules and comfort

Dogs stay leashed in shared areas, rest on their own bedding, and are welcome on the patio tables but not in the indoor restaurant room. Towels for muddy paws hang at the entrance.

## Practical notes

Mention the dog when booking so a pet-friendly floor room is assigned. A small cleaning surcharge may apply, confirmed at the front desk before arrival.
