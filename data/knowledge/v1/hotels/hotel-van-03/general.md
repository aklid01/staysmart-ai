## About the hotel

Stanley Park Lodge is a 4-star lodge-style property at the edge of Stanley Park in Vancouver's West End. The house keeps timber interiors, a pet-friendly restaurant patio and a morning breakfast room, with the park trails beginning across the street.

## Location and neighbourhood

The lodge sits between the park's forest edge and the West End's residential streets. Bikes, rollerblades and the seawall start at the corner, and downtown is a fifteen-minute walk through the trees.

## Good to know

The lodge suits outdoorsy travellers, families with dogs, and guests who want morning runs in the park. The restaurant's patio is a neighbourhood favourite on dry evenings.
