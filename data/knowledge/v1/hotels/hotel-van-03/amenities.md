## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free across the lodge. The breakfast room serves a trail-side morning spread with eggs, oats and pastries, the restaurant's timber patio serves dinner and welcomes animals at the outdoor tables, and on-site parking covers guests arriving by car. The house is pet-friendly on dedicated floors.

## Comfort details

Bike racks stand at the entrance, the front desk lends trail maps and bear bells, and a boot and paw rinse station lives by the parking gate.

## Getting around

The seawall loop starts across the street, city buses run along the park edge, and the walk to English Bay takes about ten minutes.
