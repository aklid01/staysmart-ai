## Wellness

The spa offers treatment rooms, a steam room and a quiet relaxation lounge. The outdoor pool is open in the warm months, and the walled garden around it has loungers and shade. Guests can book treatments at the spa desk.

## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free everywhere. Breakfast is a long garden-cafe affair with a full buffet. The restaurant serves dinner with a riverside Portuguese menu, and covered parking is available in the gated garage. The family wing is pet-friendly, so animals are part of daily life here.

## Services

Concierge, tour bookings and bicycle hire run from reception, and the garden cafe doubles as an all-day coffee stop.
