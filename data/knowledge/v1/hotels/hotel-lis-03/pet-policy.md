## Pets at Belem Garden Spa

Pets are allowed at this property. The family wing keeps pet-friendly rooms with direct garden access, so animals can reach the outdoors without crossing the lobby.

## Rules and comfort

Animals stay supervised in the garden, are not permitted in the restaurant or the spa, and rest in their own basket brought from home. Water bowls and a garden hose are provided in the family wing.

## Practical notes

Mention the animal when booking so a pet-friendly room in the family wing is assigned. A cleaning surcharge may apply, confirmed at the reservations desk before arrival.
