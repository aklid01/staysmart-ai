## About the hotel

Belem Garden Spa is a 5-star property in Belem, Lisbon, built around a walled garden. The house combines a full spa, an outdoor pool and a pet-friendly family wing, and it holds one of the highest guest ratings in the city.

## Location and neighbourhood

Belem is the riverside home of monasteries, museums and the famous pastry cafés. The garden gate opens towards the river walk, and the tram line into central Lisbon stops nearby.

## Good to know

The hotel suits families, couples celebrating something, and travellers who want wellness facilities on site. The family wing keeps quieter hours and welcomes animals.
