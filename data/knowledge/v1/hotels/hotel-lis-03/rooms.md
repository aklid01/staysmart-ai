## Garden Deluxe

The Garden Deluxe sleeps up to 2 guests in a king bed. It opens towards the garden with a private terrace corner, wi-fi, and breakfast served at the garden cafe. Cooling is provided by ceiling fans and opening windows in the traditional wing.

## Family Suite

The Family Suite sleeps up to 4 guests in two queen beds. It is a pet-friendly room with a separate sitting area, wi-fi, breakfast included for the whole party, and direct access to the garden.

## Which to choose

The Garden Deluxe suits couples, the Family Suite suits parents with children or guests travelling with animals. Both categories connect by an internal corridor to the spa wing.
