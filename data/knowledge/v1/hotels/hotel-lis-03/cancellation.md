## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 7 days before check-in. Cancellations made inside that window are charged less a 1-night penalty, which is taken from the total at cancellation time.

## How to cancel

Cancel through the booking channel or the reservations desk. The confirmation includes the cancellation reference and any penalty applied.

## Refund process

Refunds return to the original payment method after the cancellation is processed. The one-night penalty, when it applies, is itemised separately on the confirmation.

## No-show policy

A no-show is charged the penalty described above and the remaining nights are released back into the garden wing inventory.
