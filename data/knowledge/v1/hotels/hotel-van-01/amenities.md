## Wellness

The indoor pool runs lanes through the morning and open swim later in the day. The spa offers massages, facials and a hot-and-cold circuit, booked at the spa desk. The gym shares the pool level and opens early.

## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free throughout. The harbour-level restaurant serves dinner and weekend brunch, and covered valet parking operates from the second garage level. All rooms and shared areas have air conditioning.

## Services

The concierge arranges whale watching, seaplane tours and cruise-terminal transfers, and the business centre offers printing and courier drop-off.
