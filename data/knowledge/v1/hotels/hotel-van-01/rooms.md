## Harbour King

The Harbour King sleeps up to 2 guests in a king bed. It is a full-width room with a harbour-view window, wi-fi, air conditioning, a marble bathroom and a compact work desk with a harbour-facing chair.

## View notes

Harbour-facing rooms watch the marina and float planes; city-facing rooms on the opposite side trade the view for a slightly lower position in the house. Both keep the same fittings.

## Practical notes

Housekeeping runs daily, the pillow menu sits in the room binder, and same-day laundry collected before nine finishes by evening.
