## Pets at Coal Harbour Grand

Pets are not allowed at this property. The spa, pool and restaurant levels operate as fur-free zones and the room inventory is kept allergen-friendly.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are accommodated where required by law. Flag this during booking so the floor assignment can be arranged.

## Nearby options

The concierge keeps a list of licensed boarding and dog-day-care providers downtown for guests travelling with animals.
