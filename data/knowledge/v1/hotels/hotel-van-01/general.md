## About the hotel

Coal Harbour Grand is a 5-star waterfront business hotel in Vancouver. It combines a full-service spa, an indoor pool, a fitness centre and covered parking under one roof, with the waterfront seawall at the doorstep.

## Location and neighbourhood

Coal Harbour is the marina district between the cruise terminal and Stanley Park. The seawall walk, float planes and the downtown core are all minutes away on foot.

## Good to know

The hotel suits business travellers, spa weekends and pre-cruise stays. Guests can walk the seawall at sunset and be back for a late dinner in the harbour restaurant.
