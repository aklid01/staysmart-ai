## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 2 days before check-in. Cancel before the deadline and nothing is charged; cancellations after the deadline are charged for the first night of the stay.

## How to cancel

Cancel in the booking channel or by phone with the reservations desk. Every cancellation receives a written confirmation with a reference number.

## Refund process

Prepaid amounts return to the original payment method after the cancellation is confirmed. Processing usually completes within a few business days.

## No-show policy

A no-show without cancellation is charged for the first night and the remaining nights are released to the general inventory.
