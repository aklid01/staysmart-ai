## Compact Double

The Compact Double sleeps up to 2 guests in a double bed. It is a small room with the essentials: wi-fi, fresh linen, a wardrobe and a private shower room. There is no desk area, by design.

## What to expect

Walls are thin by budget-house standards, so light sleepers may want earplugs. Rooms face the street or the inner stairwell, and linen changes run every third day for longer stays.

## Best for

Backpackers, students and short city breaks where location matters more than fittings.
