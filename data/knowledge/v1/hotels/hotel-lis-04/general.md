## About the hotel

Chiado Budget Rooms is a 2-star budget property in a central Chiado building. It offers simple, clean rooms at the lowest price point in the Lisbon collection, which is reflected in a large volume of guest reviews.

## Location and neighbourhood

Chiado sits between Baixa and Bairro Alto. Shops, theatres and the metro are minutes away, and the neighbourhood is lively through the evening.

## Good to know

The house suits travellers who spend their days out and want an honest, central bed at night. Reception is staffed during the day, with a self-service arrival process outside those hours.
