## Cancellation terms

Bookings at Chiado Budget Rooms are non-refundable. The budget rate is final at the moment of booking and the stay cannot be cancelled, modified or refunded afterwards.

## Date changes

Moving a booking to different dates counts as a modification and is not permitted on this rate. If plans change, a new booking at the current price can be made instead.

## Exceptions

Where consumer protection rules of the booking jurisdiction require an exception, the reservation desk applies the rule and documents it on the booking record.
