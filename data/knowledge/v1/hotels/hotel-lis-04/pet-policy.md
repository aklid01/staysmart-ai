## Pets at Chiado Budget Rooms

Pets are not allowed at this property. Rooms are compact and shared walls make animal noise difficult to manage in a budget house.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are accepted where required by law; notify the property in advance so the arrival process is prepared for it.

## Nearby options

Reception can point to boarding providers in central Lisbon for guests travelling with animals.
