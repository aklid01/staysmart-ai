## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free in every room and in the entrance hall. That is deliberately the full list: the house keeps rates low by skipping extras, and the cafes of Chiado cover everything else within a short walk.

## Practical services

A laundry bag and self-service washing instructions live in each room, luggage can be left at the entrance hall on departure day, and a small shelf of traded books sits by the door.

## Getting around

The metro and the funicular are each a few minutes on foot, and the airport bus stops within walking distance.
