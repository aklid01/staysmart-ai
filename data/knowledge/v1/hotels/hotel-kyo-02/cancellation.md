## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 3 days before check-in. Cancel before that point and nothing is charged; cancellations after the deadline are charged for the first night of the stay.

## How to cancel

Cancel in the booking channel or at the front desk. A confirmation with a cancellation reference is issued every time.

## Refund process

Prepaid amounts are refunded to the original payment method after the cancellation is confirmed. Processing typically completes within a few business days.

## No-show policy

A no-show without cancellation is charged for the first night and the remaining nights are released to the general inventory.
