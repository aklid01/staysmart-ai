## City Double

The City Double sleeps up to 2 guests in a queen bed. It is an efficient business room with wi-fi, air conditioning, a work desk, blackout curtains and a view towards the station or the city grid depending on the floor.

## Sound and light

Rooms facing the station hear a gentle hum of trains; upper floors are quieter. Blackout curtains make early sunrises a non-issue for guests adjusting to Japan time.

## Practical notes

Housekeeping runs daily, same-day laundry drops finish by evening, and the front desk holds luggage for guests with late trains.
