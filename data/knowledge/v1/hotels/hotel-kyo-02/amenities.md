## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi is free throughout the tower. The gym on the top floor has cardio machines and weights with skyline views, the ground-floor restaurant serves Japanese and Western dinner menus, and a free shuttle links the hotel with the city's main exhibition centre on weekdays. Every room has air conditioning.

## Services

Coin laundry, luggage storage, a convenience counter with rail snacks, and dry-cleaning drop-off run daily. Meeting corners with power sockets sit off the lobby.

## Getting around

Kyoto Station is next door: trains, subway lines and buses leave from the same complex, and the airport express platform is a short indoor walk away.
