## About the hotel

Kyoto Station Tower is a 4-star station-side hotel with over a thousand reviews. It is the practical choice in the Kyoto collection: large, efficient, and built around fast arrivals and departures.

## Location and neighbourhood

The hotel stands next to Kyoto Station, the city's transport hub. Trains, the subway, buses and the airport express all connect at the station, and the tower's upper floors look over the city grid.

## Good to know

The hotel suits travellers with early trains, business visitors, and anyone who values transport over atmosphere. Reception runs a fast check-in lane for guests arriving by rail.
