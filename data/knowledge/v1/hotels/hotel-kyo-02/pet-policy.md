## Pets at Kyoto Station Tower

Pets are not allowed at this property. The tower's high room turnover and shared corridors make it an unsuitable environment for animals.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are accommodated where required by law. Notify the hotel at booking so a suitable floor can be assigned.

## Nearby options

The front desk keeps a list of licensed pet boarding providers near the station for guests travelling with animals, and luggage storage covers the handover hours neatly.
