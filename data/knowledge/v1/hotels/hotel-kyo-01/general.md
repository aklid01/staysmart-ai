## About the hotel

Gion Machiya Stay is a 4-star restored machiya townhouse near Gion in Kyoto. A machiya is a traditional wooden town house; this one keeps the original lattice front and wooden interior while adding modern comfort, and it holds a very high guest rating.

## Location and neighbourhood

Gion is the famous geisha district. Lanes with wooden fronts, tea houses and the Yasaka shrine area are within a short walk, and buses along the main road reach the rest of Kyoto quickly.

## Good to know

Breakfast service is part of the morning rhythm here, delivered to the townhouse. The house suits travellers who want an authentic Kyoto stay with personal service rather than a large hotel.
