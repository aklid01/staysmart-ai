## Pets at Gion Machiya Stay

Pets are allowed at this property. The townhouse layout, with its own entrance and a contained courtyard, makes it an easy stay for a well-behaved small dog or cat.

## Rules and comfort

Animals stay off the tatami in wet weather, sleep on their own bedding brought from home, and stay supervised in the courtyard. Food and water bowls are available from the house.

## Practical notes

Tell the house about the animal when booking so the ground-floor room can be assigned. A small cleaning surcharge may apply and is confirmed before arrival.
