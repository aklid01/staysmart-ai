## Cancellation terms

Free cancellation applies until 5 days before check-in. Cancellations inside that window are charged less a 1-night penalty, consistent with the house's small size where a cancelled room is hard to resell.

## How to cancel

Cancel through the booking channel or message the house directly. Every cancellation receives a written confirmation with a reference.

## Refund process

Refunds return to the original payment method after processing. When the penalty applies it is listed as a separate line on the cancellation confirmation.

## No-show policy

A no-show is charged the one-night penalty and the remaining nights are released for other guests.
