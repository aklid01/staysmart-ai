## Tatami Double

The Tatami Double sleeps up to 2 guests on futon bedding laid out over tatami mats in the evening. The room keeps a low wooden table, floor cushions, wi-fi, air conditioning, and a breakfast tray service each morning.

## Sleeping on futons

Futons are aired daily and laid out while guests are at dinner. Guests who have never slept on one should expect a firm, floor-level bed that many find excellent for backs.

## House rhythm

Shoes come off at the entrance, and the small garden courtyard is open to guests in the evening.
