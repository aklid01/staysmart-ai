## Hotel facilities

Wi-fi reaches every room and the courtyard. A Japanese-style breakfast is served each morning, either in the small dining room or as a tray in the room, and air conditioning keeps the wooden townhouse comfortable through Kyoto's humid summer.

## Comfort details

Yukata robes, tea sets and a shared tea corner are part of the stay. The house is pet-friendly, so a well-behaved animal can stay in the ground-floor room with its own bedding. The staff can arrange bicycle hire, and umbrellas stand by the entrance for rainy days.

## Getting around

Buses on the main street connect to Kyoto Station and the northern temples. The Gion lanes themselves are best on foot, and the house lends paper maps marked with the staff's favourites.
