## Check-in

Check-in starts at 15:00 for the Lisbon hotels in this collection. Arriving guests can leave luggage at reception before the room is ready, and the front desk keeps it tagged until return. Passports or national ID cards are required at arrival for all adult guests.

## Check-out

Check-out is by 11:00 on the departure day. Late departures can be requested at reception the evening before and are granted subject to availability. After check-out, luggage storage remains open for the rest of the departure day at no charge.

## Local notes

Lisbon hotels use local time, which is Western European Time. City tax is collected at the front desk at arrival and is payable by card or cash. Trams and funiculars are the fastest way uphill with luggage-free hands.
