## Check-in

Check-in starts at 16:00 for the Vancouver hotels in this collection, slightly later than most destinations to allow full servicing after cruise-season departures. Luggage storage is open from the morning, and photo identification is required for every adult guest at arrival.

## Check-out

Check-out is by 11:00 on the departure day. Late check-out can be requested at the front desk the evening before and is granted subject to availability. Guests with evening flights can leave bags in storage and use the day as they wish.

## Local notes

Vancouver uses Pacific Time. Parking in the downtown core is mostly underground and paid, so guests with cars should confirm garage arrangements with their hotel. The seawall and park trails are rain-friendly year round; umbrellas are worth packing.
