## Check-in

Check-in starts at 15:00 for the Kyoto hotels in this collection. Guests present passports at the front desk, as Japanese lodging law requires identification from every adult staying overnight. Luggage can be stored at reception until the room is ready.

## Check-out

Check-out is by 10:00 on the departure day, the standard rhythm for Japanese properties. Late check-out can be requested the evening before and is granted subject to availability. Luggage forwarding services at the station ship bags between cities if the departure flight leaves late.

## Local notes

Kyoto runs on Japan Standard Time. Slippers replace shoes indoors in machiya and ryokan stays, and cash remains useful for small shops although cards are widely taken. Trash separation follows the neighbourhood rules posted in each room.
